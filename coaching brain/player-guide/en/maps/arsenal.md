## P1

> P1 lasts 60 s: the *start-of-hill setup* and the *mid-hill play* below are advice for **different moments.**

### A) Start of the hill: head-glitch standoff `S15 · Oct 13, vs UD` ✅

**Situation:** The first seconds of P1; both sides hold a standoff at the head glitches.

▶ **Do:** **"If nobody says push, nobody pushes."** Prepare for the corner gunfight first; push when the signal comes. The ideal signal is the **SKS one-tap** (the SKS player calls it); the SMGs all know a push may come. **Smoke slightly on our side**, not on their close head glitch, to hide your body from their AR. **If our AR is winning against their AR → swing. If our AR is dying → don't.** If our AR dies first and their SMG swings, the surviving SMG's job is to peek / hide at a corner **without showing your body to their AR** (make them useless and even the fight); when our AR respawns and rejoins, it's 5 vs their 2 ARs at the head glitch → swing and prepare for their second wave (SMG). The coach calls it **tempo management.**

✖ **Don't:** Swing on your own judgment (you can't tell if their open player is an AR or an SMG). Don't "play safe for no reason" by putting all 5 in the hill. One example: a lone push to the right after a kill, with no info shared, and the only teammates alive couldn't help.

### B) Five players in the hill is over-investment `S15` ✅

**Situation:** You are setting up P1.

▶ **Do:** *"Putting five players is kind of an overinvestment in P1. So somebody has to wrap outside."* Send **one** player outside; the rest in the hill swing as pairs.

### C) Mid-hill: don't wait at the entrance `S13 · Sep 30` ✅

**Situation:** Everyone waits at the door for the enemy to pick someone, and one player throws smoke and dives alone → dies with no trade. *(A player's observation; the coach agreed: "that is one thing that we have been trying to fix".)*

▶ **Do:** If you threw smoke, or **two or more teammates are with you → go forward.** Push **straight into the hill and hold after you take it** (SKS excepted). The team needs a dedicated **hard OBJ SMG.**

✖ **Don't:** Hold by shooting from the door — that's **bait** (if you die, no one can trade). **Don't try to "take window side first"** — if both sides rush equally, it's luck. Assume the worst (someone is there) and prepare the gunfight at the corner.

### D) Equalizer now `S13` ✅ *(meta as of S13)*

**Situation:** All teammates alive and the enemy is contesting.

▶ **Do:** It's the **most valuable moment** to use the Equalizer. Don't scrub left and right separately: put the equalizer + yourself on **one side (left)**, erase it fast, then turn the firepower to the right. UD is following Elevate-style play, so you need the **final blow.**

### E) Smoke and trophy must match the route `S11 · Sep 17` 🔶 *(map inferred)*

**Situation:** The smoke goes into the hill / onto the cut.

▶ **Do:** Smoke into the hill → contest from green box side while one holds the cut; smoke on the cut → go down the cut. **Where you use smoke and trophy must match the route.**

✖ **Don't:** Throw all trophies to the right and leave the left empty — the enemy walked up for free.

### F) A single enemy hiding in white side `S15` ✅

**Situation:** One enemy shows their position at white side but does nothing — confusing and splitting you (we put two players + Claw on white while the real enemies were at main side).

▶ **Do:** **Ignore them.** If they stretch out, shooting them or going back to white are both fine; while they stay at white, don't care. What you want is to **slam and push harder toward their spawn to flip the spawn** (they spawn here, not us) — whichever side you start P1 from, push to the P3 side and ignore white.

✖ **Don't:** Move to a different spot because of a white call. Taking white isn't a guaranteed win either: the enemy can hide in vent / head glitch / statue. **Pick one and commit.**

### G) SMG head glitch peek `S15` ✅ *(Switchblade meta as of S15)*

**Situation:** A teammate already cleared the angle with a slide; the head glitch is empty.

▶ **Do:** Move **at once** to the free side and stick to the wall to avoid the SKS angle inside. Worried? Jiggle peek. **Move wall to wall.**

✖ **Don't:** ADS-peek the same angle, and don't spend time in the gap between walls. Don't peek + move with a Switchblade open to two angles — their AR is fully ready; you won't win.

## P2

### A) Yellow side priority `S15` ✅

**Situation:** You just got a double kill (or a kill) near P2.

▶ **Do:** **Make your priority clear:** when you have a chance, **physically step on yellow side**, and hunt the enemy who killed you at once (trade). A player exposed after two kills is hunted immediately (UD is fast).

✖ **Don't:** Split your attention reacting to an enemy who spotted your spawn / back (SMG "comfort zone": easy slay out — "that is not what we're trying to do right now"). Don't blame unclear calls every time.

### B) P1 → P2 rotation: don't get stuck on yellow `S15` ✅ *(coach confirmed Oct 15: this is about the P1 → P2 rotation)*

**Situation:** You wrap outside to take yellow (the P2 spawn) while the enemy still eats the P1 scrap.

▶ **Do:** Rotate, but **don't be obsessed with taking yellow as fast as possible** — P2's yellow spawn is **less stable** than you think. *"It is not about speed. It is not about how correct we do."* Expect the enemy to take the same outside route.

✖ **Don't:** Always play it that way: the enemy eating the P1 scrap can **cut** you while you wrap.

### C) Rotation vs scrap — one or the other `S15` ✅

**Situation:** The hill is ending (e.g. ~10 s left). *Scrap* = the remaining points of the previous hill (coach-confirmed definition).

▶ **Do:** If the team is **rotating, drop the scrap**; if the team goes for scrap, everybody goes — especially the **last player to respawn.** Spawned at yellow side? Rotate, but a full scrap is fine too.

✖ **Don't:** Split the team physically (one to the old hill, one to the new). That is what to avoid.

### D) Losing → rotate before you hunt kills `S18 · Oct 23` ✅ *(review of the Oct 22 scrim; coach confirmed Arsenal Oct 24)*

**Situation:** You're losing P1 hard, or about to give a whole hill away.

▶ **Do:** **Rotate earlier** to cut the momentum and set up — *"we promised to focus on the rotation first".* Take the tunnel if you're losing. Rotating early keeps the team together (a 2v1 survives); you get to the yellow side and **set up first to turn it around.** **Say it while rotating:** the coach's biggest point was that nobody *spoke.* If you're **ahead on points** and the next hill is hard to break, giving up the scrap (e.g. 17 s) and rotating first is "no big deal."

✖ **Don't:** Fight pointless gunfights at P1 main, or push a P1 hill that has 5 s left. Don't start "finding some kills first" when you fall behind — some do and some don't, the team splits, and you forget the priority.

## P4

### A) Yellow spawn: pick one and say it `S13 · Sep 30` ✅

**Situation:** *"This is the hill that players get nated the most."* You are already inside the hill and want the yellow spawn.

▶ **Do:** **Pick one.** Tell each other you're pushing **elbow side / yellow side** and take the initiative. Communication isn't "do this" — it's **sharing what you're about to do.** Here you can push: you're **already inside**, it's clear which angle to peek, and you have two players.

✖ **Don't:** Wait for the enemy to walk into your crosshair. *(It looks like a contradiction with P2, where good kill feed was a signal to solidify — the context differs.)*

### B) Rotation and nade tips `S15` ✅

**Situation:** Rotating on P4 (white → P1 → window) and throwing grenades.

▶ **Do:** **Memorize the rotation:** if you come by staircase, you overlap with a teammate already using it. After arriving at window, peeking is fine. For nades: don't cook too long; **the angle matters** — a throw tends to explode mid-air, get blocked by a trophy, or hit the map edge / a barrel. The goal is to land **inside the hill** to suppress / delay so teammates inside win more fights.

✖ **Don't:** Peek the right head glitch angle carelessly. (A bold peek that fails is "worth trying" — not blamed.)

## Any hill

- **After a 3-kill `S13` ✅:** it's good for the player but they are **most vulnerable** — three enemies call their position and their next-kill chance keeps dropping. The team mistakenly thinks "three are dead, let's expand", but a 3-kill player must be **filled in at once.** If he's one-shot after the kills, *"he used all of his stamina"* (the coach's words) → **every priority is helping that player** (trade kills; others stay ready for that player's condition rather than focusing on what they can do). The enemy only pushes when it must and slams with numbers. Killing one enemy at elbow side has small value unless you return to the hill at once (the callout reveals your position and the enemies inside switch to protecting their lives).
- **2 kills + near the hill → live (SMG) `S15` ✅:** *"You'll be the most hated guy in that situation … but if you're staying alive, play for the life, then your teammate got you."* After a few kills there is a point where **survival time matters more.**
- **Don't expand out of annoyance `S15` ✅:** *"what I'm afraid of is those kind of thinking process could be a habit."* (Example: going outside to stick a Tempest on an enemy who has no time to place a trophy.)
- **Equalizer `S15` ✅:** use it defensively as told; it's random, so don't worry about it and keep using it.
