# Team Glossary

> 📌 This guide is **extracted from Coach Flower's session transcripts** — a summary, not new rules. The coach's advice depends on **map, hill, situation, opponent and timing**: read the *Situation* line first.

**Confidence:** ✅ coach said it · 🔶 inferred / not confirmed by the coach yet — treat as "maybe" · ❓ unconfirmed — **not an instruction** (see the last section).
**Tags:** `S19` = Session 19 (review of a past scrim). Dates are shown where the knowledge base has them.

> The words the coach uses and the team shares, so VODs and live coaching speak one language. Game terms stay in English. ## Mindset

- **Play to win / Play not to lose** — challenge to win, versus defending only so you don't lose. In CODM the first is the winning play. `S6`
- **Keep your intention** — once you set an intention, keep it. In a pinch don't drop the original goal. `S6`
- **Intention first** — decide the intention first: if it's spawn, go for the spawn; if it's the hill, go for the hill. `S13`
- **Unify thinking** — when the whole team makes the same judgment (e.g. "3 kills → time to enter"), one person choosing differently splits the team's thinking. `S9`
- **Lazy assumption** — "the enemy will walk into my crosshair" / "a teammate must be somewhere". `S13 · S14`
- **Assume the worst** — assume a teammate won't manage it (so help them) and that a corner has an enemy (so prepare the gunfight). `S11 · S13`
- **Valuing yourself too much** — overrating what you can do outside, and leaving (P2 snow / CDOM slay out). `S16`
- **Do everything → do nothing** — one person watching CDOM + flank + hill support ends up doing none. `S16`
- **Tunnel vision** — hearing the call and still not doing what you must. Physical; needs a conscious effort. It won't be fixed in a day or two, but visible effort counts as improvement. `S16`
- **One mistake per mode** — pick *one* mistake per mode and focus on it all week (HP = rotation). `S16`
- **Common-sense illusion** — what's obvious with your info isn't for your teammate; don't do it silently and then blame them with "it was common sense." `S18`
- **Say what you're doing** — a *statement* of what you're about to do, not an order. It's info for teammates and a way to get help; teammates don't have to follow. `S18`
- **Say it, mean it** — "I'll kill him" is a good call; once said, finish it. `S17`
- **Habit** — correct gunfight *habits* consciously (not aim); POV recording helps. `S9 · S10`
- **Don't solve it by killing** — trying to solve a problem you read by getting a kill (overconfidence in gunfights; principle 10). `S10`
- **Last resort (AR)** — an SMG may die, an AR shouldn't: it's the team's last resource. `S9`

## Value and positioning

- **Money hill** — the hill with the largest effect on score on a map (e.g. Combine P3). `S3`
- **Hill relation (거점과의 연관성)** 🔶 — keep your physical distance to the hill very short (Takeoff). You don't have to step in, but stay within reach. `S7`
- **Value of a death (16 s / 4 s)** 🔶 — a kill/death inside the hill is worth 16 s, outside only 4 s: inside is heavy. `S7`
- **Slay illusion (슬레이 환상)** 🔶 — overrating your own death, going to slay and dying, ignoring the hill. You must admit a death is cheap to be able to challenge. `S7`
- **100% hit (100% 안타)** 🔶 — don't spread value over three spots (33 % × 3); commit 100 % to one. `S7`
- **Kinetic-energy focus (운동 에너지 집중)** 🔶 — at peak potential energy, put everything into the single most likely spot and ignore the other candidates. `S7`
- **Brain freeze (내정지)** 🔶 — a state of trying to watch too many angles, caused by anxiety. Give up some angles and restart from a safe spot. `S7`
- **Pick one / Focus and select (선택과 집중)** — even if several angles make sense, pick one and ignore the rest; "what you need is movement, not aim." `S7 · S9`
- **Potential value** — the value of the player in the best position; use it to help the team, not for kills. `S5 · S6`
- **Shadow / Imaginary enemy** — don't reveal yourself; be the enemy's imaginary enemy. `S1`
- **Bleeding** — slowly wearing the enemy down (time and information edge). `S1`
- **Cycling out / Cycling secret** — leaving the hill to chase kills outside (a bad habit). `S1`
- **Slay out** — going out of the hill for kills; has little value without hill control. `S5`
- **Shoulder to shoulder** — all 5 aligned at once; a substitute for the "correct play." `S4`
- **Front line / Anchor / Deep** — forward / middle / deep positions in a site, by role. `S1 · S4`
- **Power position** — a position structurally advantageous for gunfights. `S8`
- **Route, not goal** — top P2, CDOM, snow help the hill as *routes*; they're not *destinations* to clean. `S15 · S16`
- **Value test** — to judge a zone's value, ask *"what happens if the enemy uses that zone?"* `S16`
- **Top illusion** — 30–40 % of the value of Summit P1 top P2 is an illusion; even that lets the enemy break P1. `S17`
- **Pick one at top** — the top P2 player picks staircase peek **or** window → hill; if split, window. `S17`
- **Lock** — locking the hill (fixing the score) — a spawn decision. `S3`
- **Bait** — a decoy play: the longer you live the more annoying you are, but corners are read first. `S10`
- **Tie them down** — show your body without dying so one enemy keeps looking at you (Summit P4 main side forklift guy). `S9`
- **Tanking util** — absorbing the enemy's util up front. `S4`
- **Beached (좌초)** 🔶 — staying about a minute in a non-power position and giving up the lane; "sitting" itself is the problem (Raid mid stairs). `S8`

## Hardpoint terms

- **Recycle the hill** — a flow of spawned players continuously entering the hill; holders get ready too. **Definition (updated):** not "fill when empty" but **go in even if someone is already inside** — by the time you hear a call or see the OBJ die, it's late. `S12 · S16`
- **Flooding (the enemy's)** — the enemy keeps pouring into the hill; whoever is set inside first has the edge. `S16`
- **Scrap (point)** — the **remaining points on the previous hill** when both teams should be moving to the next one (e.g. ~10 s left) — coach confirmed. "Rotation vs scrap" = rotate to the next hill or eat the remaining time. `S15`
- **Rotation first** — when losing, the promised priority is to rotate early so the team stays together; when ahead on points you may give up the scrap. `S18`
- **Pace** — the whole team deciding and executing at the same speed. `S13`
- **Overinvestment** — putting all 5 on a low-value hill (P1); one must wrap outside. `S15`
- **Wrap outside** — taking a big loop outside to hit the enemy spawn / flank. In P1 → P2 rotation, getting stuck on grabbing yellow lets the enemy's P1 scrap cut you. `S15`
- **Play for life** — an SMG with 2 kills near the hill doesn't expand; they stay alive. `S15`
- **Comfort zone (SMG)** — reacting to an easy-kill spot (e.g. staircase) and missing your priority. `S12 · S15`
- **Support line (AR)** — the AR flow that keeps filling computer side etc. so the SMG is backed up. `S9 · S15`
- **Push signal** — don't swing on your own judgment; push only on a signal from a chosen player (e.g. an SKS one-tap). "If nobody says push, nobody pushes." `S15`
- **Priority clear** — when you can, step on yellow side and hunt the enemy who killed you for a trade. `S15`
- **Final blow** — finishing the enemy for sure to keep the gap; if you don't, they buy operator time. `S10`
- **Solidify vs Expand** — protect what you have (OBJ, position) and land final blows, versus expanding outside. The goal at this stage is to solidify. `S10–S13`
- **3-piece and the stamina model** — the more kills, the lower the next-kill chance; right after 3 kills is the most vulnerable → fill in immediately. `S13`
- **Stamina (a teammate's)** — someone who had a big solo run is near their limit; swap in at once. `S9`
- **Full 60** — eating the entire 60 s of a hill. Don't just hold after pouring operators; push out if you can. `S17`
- **Second chance (rock)** — rock + statue for 60 s at once is impossible; get the advantage, step back, use rock again. `S17`
- **Lane control first (defense)** — on defense, don't rush to retake; control the lane, farm kills, then move. `S17`
- **Mid as hub** — on defense mid is the relay to send players to B, A and the mid stairs. `S17`
- **Sponge** — absorb a point the enemy took (P5) and farm kills in the zones where you prefer to fight, instead of hurrying to retake. `S17`
- **Hunt the known guy** — hunt one enemy whose position you know before pushing a lane. `S17`
- **String them out / pinned** — a call that pushes the enemy out; breaks the situation where you outnumber them / have operators but are pinned for no reason. `S16`
- **End-point call** — call not only the route but what you'll do at the end (kill or pull out). `S16`
- **Mirror reasoning** — what scares you most on defense (a 4-player slam on P3 from main), do it on attack. `S18`
- **Operator gap** — even a perfect setup breaks when the enemy uses an operator inside. `S11`
- **Wasted firepower** — ARs stacked on the same spot and blocked by smoke → reposition to another route. `S17`
- **Smoke ownership** — who throws which smoke where and who benefits: **no agreement yet** (players' discussion). `S17`
- **Wait for the IGL call** — having no plan of your own at round start and only waiting for the IGL. `S17`

## Operators and tactics

- **Sacrifice / Promise** — when an operator is used, one player enters as a trade; agreed beforehand. `S6`
- **See, then pop** — operators are used after confirming, not guessing. `S5 · S6`
- **Undodgeable** — a situation the enemy can't avoid; where operators shine. `S5`
- **Pinch** — being surrounded from both sides; the moment keeping your intention matters most. `S6`
- **Slam** — a rush where the enemy comes in all at once; the best timing for War Machine / Tempest. `S6`
- **Trade kill** — your death is traded by a teammate's kill; the standard for a valuable death. `S5 · S6`
- **Bother** — a lone player who doesn't die and harasses the enemy to distract them. `S6`
- **Nade habit (폭 근)** 🔶 — throwing a grenade as a habit; on P3 / P4 *not* throwing should feel unnatural. Build it as muscle memory. `S7`
- **Mappings** — documented per-map game plans (the coach will do it with all players present). `S5`
- **Going x3** — a proposed pre-entry call repeated three times to align timing. *(a proposal)*

## SnD and Control

- **Lurker (designated flank)** — the player who handles the flank from the start of the round; may show their position on purpose to confuse. `S15`
- **Clear before plant** — plant when the site clear is 100 %; if there's time, don't hurry. `S16`
- **One behind** — in a 4–5 player push/rush, keep one player behind to read the enemy reflank and keep the information edge. `S18`
- **Solo chow** — a lone death; when they pile up, fatal (Raid 13–16 loss). `S8`
- **Map control > lane monopoly** — don't force one lane; take site + mid and widen the ticket gap with trades. `S8`
- **Prefire (assume top red always)** — remove the top red AR you spot before entering main. `S8`

## Map-specific

- **CDOM** (Summit) — the zone matching Domination's "C" point; low cleaning value in P1 / P2, priority in P4 — **depends on the hill**. `S16`
- **Elevate** — the 2024 World Championship–winning team (coach confirmed Oct 17); UD imitates it. `S16`
