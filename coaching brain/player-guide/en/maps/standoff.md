## Control

### B push is the key `S5` ✅ *(the coach's drawing, explained)*

**Situation:** You attack Standoff in Control. **B push is the key (A push is fine).**

▶ **Do:** Build the chain: for B you need **statue control**; for statue you need **broken control**; for broken there are two routes — **(1)** take A site and push, **(2)** hide behind a vehicle and peek left. To control broken you must **block the top granny sight line** (a sniper watches top granny).
- **Smoke statue + 2 tractor + 2 mid:** 3 players smoke statue and push, 1 watches control, the sniper holds top granny.
- **Top market** has low early value, so the enemy doesn't put anyone there — unless they run a special SKS / sniper tactic.

✖ **Don't:** Try a B push without statue / broken control — you get deleted from the angles. *(Common mistake.)*

### Sparrow idea (Standoff B only) `S5` 🔶 *(a coach proposal; player pushback expected)*

**Situation:** You need to clear B.

▶ **Do:** Instead of the death machine, bombard B with a **Sparrow** → clear → retake.

✖ **Don't:** Carry this idea to other maps: it's limited to Standoff B (the coach can't imagine a Sparrow on Raid). *Meta-dependent — the weapon situation changes over time.*
