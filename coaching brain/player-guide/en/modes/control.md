# Control — General Principles

> 📌 This guide is **extracted from Coach Flower's session transcripts** — a summary, not new rules. The coach's advice depends on **map, hill, situation, opponent and timing**: read the *Situation* line first.

**Confidence:** ✅ coach said it · 🔶 inferred / not confirmed by the coach yet — treat as "maybe" · ❓ unconfirmed — **not an instruction** (see the last section).
**Tags:** `S19` = Session 19 (review of a past scrim). Dates are shown where the knowledge base has them.

> Capture / defend sites with a **ticket** (life count) system. 2026 map pool: Raid, Standoff, Crossroads Strike — each has its own page. Control is a **challenge** mode; Hardpoint is a **support** (hill-centered) mode `S10`.

---

## 1. Play to win, not to not-lose `S5` ✅

**Situation:** You have enough tickets.

▶ **Do:** Try to attack. Dying while charging and making trades is worth more than dying while holding. **Force the active attack:** with or without momentum, at some point you must push boldly — **stay close to your teammates** (no random exploring). A death that makes a **trade kill** gives the team info; a death while holding gives nothing (principle 7).

✖ **Don't:** Repeat worthless deaths ("holding until you die").

---

## 2. Spawn kills and the support line `S10` ✅

**Situation:** You're in an attack and a teammate is being spawn-killed or is far ahead.

▶ **Do:** Spawn-killing is **one of the most effective ways** to control the map and tickets, so players must **keep flowing toward the spawn.** If you've been alive for a long time, teammates are ahead or behind you: **help the one in front first** (e.g. the one capturing B). If you can't help right away, **say whether you can or not** so they can decide to save their life. A teammate dying behind you will respawn anyway while tickets remain ("trust our teammates"). **The way to save a spawn-killed teammate is to go into the site:** once you start capturing, the enemy must give up the position and come to help the point, so the spawn kill stops. If you're the one furthest ahead: **a strong support line** beats a forced push — hide info and farm kills in the tank area; push only when 100 % sure. *"Don't die until your teammate comes."* If a teammate needs help, go **immediately.**

✖ **Don't:** Play it like Hardpoint (circling the hill and slamming works only when the enemy is desperate for the hill). In Control the enemy keeps slamming from outside; you lose timing and die by accident. **In Control, challenge to earn space.** `S10`

---

## 3. War Machine in Control `S5` ✅

**Situation:** You hold a War Machine for Control (the example was a sniper player).

▶ **Do:** Let the enemy **come in**, then bombard in an **undodgeable** situation. The right time is when the enemy is down to **6** players and plays for lives (tries to break through with operators). Use it from a safe distance (statue side, or inside the hill).

✖ **Don't:** Use it early to defend B site — the enemy will dodge. Once they know about the War Machine, they throw a trophy and avoid it, so pre-using it is pointless.

---

## 4. Common mistakes (checklist)

- Using an operator in advance to defend B site → the enemy dodges → waste `S5`.
- Dying while holding, again and again `S5`.
- Staying beached for a long time in non-power positions (Raid mid stairs) → giving the lane away `S8`.
- Chasing spawn kills while ahead on tickets → kills go up, map control is lost `S8`.
- Rushing to retake a point the enemy took (P5 / site) while defending → losing lane control `S17`.
- Saying "I'll kill him" and not finishing it, each assuming the other did `S17`.
