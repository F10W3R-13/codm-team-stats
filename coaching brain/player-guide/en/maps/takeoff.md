## P1

**Situation:** The opponent has map control and is leading the kill feed.

▶ **Do:** Don't look for kills outside. If you hold top P1 / top P2, that space is enough. *Different case:* when your team holds the OBJ, trades are fair and all 5 are alive — that is the moment to expand. `S14 · Oct 7` 🔶

✖ **Don't:** Push for more space or hunt kills (ticket side in P1, deep stadium side in P2) — enemies sitting in corners keep killing you. `S14` 🔶

> *Initial / P1 priority for the team's P3 plan = catwalk — see P3.* `S10` ✅

---

## P2

**Situation:** You are inside P2 hill. The entrances are small and spawns are easy to read, but inside there are almost no corners or head glitches to hide in.

▶ **Do:** Pick **one** of the two main entrances and move left–right **together as a team**, so that entrance gets kills guaranteed. Farm those kills to collect operator skills for the next wave (the white side spawn). Be active: *the team that initiates is the team that can react.* `S19 · Oct 30` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Spread out to cover every angle and wait for enemies to walk into your crosshair. Don't open P2 trying to take white side with operators from the start. `S19` ✅

💡 **Why:** With enough kills and the next wave covered, the setup can push the enemy to spawn at **secret side**. *"Having a proper P2 is the beginning of a good P3 — I cannot separate P2, P3 and P4."* `S19` ✅

## P3

### A) You hold P3

**Situation:** You hold P3 — most true when you have time and space.

▶ **Do:**
- **Top red first.** Holding top red makes holding P3 easier. `S5` ✅
- Team plan: **secure catwalk → focus the hill → defend top red.** `S10` ✅
- First kill = the enemy inside the hill who rotated first. After a kill at lounge, move toward the hill (closer to spawn = closer to your team). `S5` ✅
- Rotated early? Stay **inside** the hill, and go straight in right after you spawn. `S19` ✅
- At top red, **life > kill.** After killing a Claw user, stop there (even if teammates already push main) — hiding for 30 s is fine. No info and must enter? Prefire the closest angle. `S14` 🔶
- Help what teammates are already doing. `S10` ✅

✖ **Don't:**
- Stretch out, or park at lounge worrying about the flank. `S5` ✅
- Assume "someone already cleared it" and farm free kills from your comfort zone — this split the team and gave the enemy a 3-kill. `S10` ✅
- Slide out to peek right after you spawn, or assume the enemy spawns on the opposite side — they can spawn close. `S19` ✅

*2nd P3 and you spawn at catwalk:* it can't be avoided — accept some movement from the lounge enemy. `S5` ✅

### B) The opponent holds P3 and you have numbers

**Situation:** You need to break P3 and you have the numbers. `S18 · Oct 22 scrim review`

▶ **Do:** **Slam main with numbers — and keep doing it until it works.** Get into the hill so they must spawn inside. Spawned at launch pad while they spawn at medic side? Look at medic side the moment you spawn. After reaching main, push toward the **catwalk** side, not launch. Meanwhile look for ways to use top red, but the end result is **forcing the gunfight.** ✅

✖ **Don't:** Loop around P1 to flank (it takes too long and they read it). Don't think of spawns as 50/50. Don't drop the plan after one failed push. ✅

💡 **Why (mirror logic):** What scares you most when *defending* P3 is four enemies shooting from main. **So do it to them.** It's uncomfortable (same as Summit P3), but sometimes it's the only way. ✅

## P4

### A) You hold P4

**Situation:** You are on P4. Spawns on this map are nasty, so you must stay with the hill.

▶ **Do:** Stay **physically very close to the hill** (you don't have to step in, but be within reach). If you stay on the hill well, you can answer sudden entries. Slay out only **after** you control the hill. `S7 / S5` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Stretch out just because you feel safe. Don't try to watch every angle — that's "brain freeze". Give up some angles and restart from a safe spot (back to a wall, watching one side). `S5 / S7` ✅

### B) You rotated late and the opponent took P4 from 60 s

**Situation:** You are late; the opponent got P4 from the start. `S19 · Oct 30`

▶ **Do:** **Give up the first 20–30 s, then take the rest with ONE clean push** — time the P1-side push and the main push together. Focus on enemies at the desk / close walls, not the farthest one. Put an **AR** on the P1 side rather than hoping an SMG wins — the goal is to *keep sending people to pinch*, not "only the AR can go". ✅

*Added by a player in the review (coach didn't repeat it as a rule):* stay at **full HP** (a one-shot dies to a prefire); when breaking P1, use the **right** side too, not only left. 🔶

✖ **Don't:** Rush, or throw your body in without thinking — the enemy will lock it with an operator. Don't push by prone; wait. P3 is chaos (go open gunfights); **P4 is not — stay calm.** ✅

## Any hill

### AR positioning `S8` ✅ *(hill not specified)*

**Situation:** You are the AR.

▶ **Do:** Hold **stadium side** as long as you can (late rotators take ticket / brick / medic side; stadium side also avoids FSS Hurricane's instant-kill risk — *meta as of Session 8*). After your first kill, **pause** — don't push the joystick forward; being alive is already pressure. Assume an AR is always at top red: **prefire it** before entering main.

✖ **Don't:** Throw your body at the hill — your job is to hold angles, survive and deal mid/long-range damage. Don't open up right after a kill: that's an unnecessary death.

### You reach ATM side `S14` 🔶

**Situation:** You reach ATM side — a big chance to break the hill.

▶ **Do:** Don't enter. Stay alive, hold the angle, and **pick up kills** (don't go hunting). Tell the team by voice you're at ATM: the enemy waits, ticket-side players get pinned, and the other four can slam. If you get revealed, flee **in your original momentum direction** (stadium, pillar, teammates).

✖ **Don't:** Flee to a spot where nobody can trade you (like ATM) when you've been hit — they are already prefiring it. Speed is not the point.
