## ❓ B attack idea — a player discussion, not an instruction

**Idea (speaker unknown, `S17`):** use a stun / smoke to block the side, and while one teammate holds the side, **the others go straight into B's "P1 position" and plant.** Even if someone is out on the side, a teammate can still get the kill; and even if someone dies, the team gains the position info. If the players at trash can get inside P1 and win the gunfight, **you only need to watch the flank through blue.* (The speaker also said that explaining the position out loud makes the info from moving useless — captions unclear.)

**Where general SnD principles from this map live:** waiting for orders (waiting for Cartels' call), post-plant variations and the SKS weapon / role debate — `S17`; designated lurker and answering the enemy flanker — `S15` (all in the SnD guide).
