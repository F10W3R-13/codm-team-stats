# Search & Destroy (SnD) — General Principles

> 📌 This guide is **extracted from Coach Flower's session transcripts** — a summary, not new rules. The coach's advice depends on **map, hill, situation, opponent and timing**: read the *Situation* line first.

**Confidence:** ✅ coach said it · 🔶 inferred / not confirmed by the coach yet — treat as "maybe" · ❓ unconfirmed — **not an instruction** (see the last section).
**Tags:** `S19` = Session 19 (review of a past scrim). Dates are shown where the knowledge base has them.

> Plant / defend the bomb. Rounds, **no respawn.** The most **language-dependent** mode — mic communication is a must. These are principles that hold across maps (Sessions 15–19); **map-specific advice is in the map pages** (Firing Range, Coastal, Slums, Raid). Player-by-player assignments are in the team notes. SnD maps without a page yet: Tunisia, Meltdown.

## 1. Calls and communication

**Situation:** You make a call, or you're waiting for one.

▶ **Do:** **Call the end point, not just the route:** "what will I do at the end — go for the kill or pull out?" Teammates need that *final intention* to know if they can help. A call like "I'll take the kill" when you're sure is a good call `S16` ✅. **A call is not a fixed script:** don't take a call like "302" or "311" literally and keep sitting there; you can say "go mid" in the middle of the round `S19` ✅ *(the coach: "I don't think it has anything to do with calls.")*.

✖ **Don't:** Rely on adrenaline: about **80 %** of jump-in plays are adrenaline — they work and get kills, but at close range with an SMG the teammates can't match your move. It works **against UD**; as a habit it will be punished harder by opponents with better aim `S16` ✅.

## 2. Designated lurker and the enemy flanker

**Situation:** The enemy always has one player flanking from the start of the round (UD does this, attacking or defending, until it stops working and then moves that player to the main line) `S15 · Oct 13` ✅.

▶ **Do:** **Name a dedicated flank player.** Their job is to stay far from the main group, watch, give info, and when it doesn't work, return and add chaos. **Don't always hide — sometimes show your position on purpose** to confuse the enemy: if they see you in two different areas they feel surrounded and panic, even when it's not a real pinch. *(Example: four players on the B side not pushing, one goes in A, shoots and stays alive — the enemy decides "they are everywhere".)* When the lurker calls, the other four judge if they can help `S16`. Information exchange is the key.

✖ **Don't:** Make the team watch both sides because you don't know if their flanker is flank or main support. Don't just hold the position waiting for the enemy to peek — **they have no reason to peek** while a teammate keeps flanking.

## 3. Attack: clear first, plant later

**Situation:** You are on the attack, e.g. B site with 37 s left.

▶ **Do:** **Clear first.** Plant is *not* something to hurry with 37 s left — **clear the site to 100 %** first `S16` ✅. How to clear tires: the carrier shouldn't go straight to cubby but **wrap the outside lane and look at tires first**, giving the sniper side free space; a teammate pairs with the carrier to peek tires; another watches above. If the smoke ramp was thrown, everybody sweeps the tires; **jiggle peek first** instead of a full ADS peek with no info, and go on "one, two, three" together. **Long rush → one behind:** when you do a big wrap / 4–5 player push, leave **one player behind** to read the reflank (their way to hit our back is short and easy) — even if you're down to four `S18` ✅. If the enemy keeps one behind and all 5 of ours do the same thing, "they just know" — the player behind keeps info and initiative even if they die. **Post-plant:** call micro plays between two players ("come with me, let's win A bar"), and don't be in the same spot every round — the enemy noticed `S17` 🔶.

✖ **Don't:** Over-prefire (it killed two in the example) or leave a teammate stunned and frozen. Don't use the same post-plant position every round.

## 4. Defense: keep the numbers

**Situation:** You defend, and the enemy keeps slamming with a number push.

▶ **Do:** **The first fix is unnecessary deaths.** *"As long as we avoid getting slammed by their number push, we'll be fine."* Most enemy first / second kills happen because we sat where we **couldn't escape.** Defense's edge is *information* (1-2-1 or any spread), but a player in a spot when 5 enemies enter the site doesn't live. Calling positions doesn't help — "everyone knows they're inside the B site once they get in." So **keep your numbers from places that have an escape route**; if you must be in such a spot, **hide until something happens** `S19` ✅. See Firing Range and Coastal.

## 5. Sniper, roles and util

**Situation:** You use the sniper as a weapon, or you decide who carries which util.

▶ **Do:** **A sniper is a weapon you ask for:** the other four must know the sniper's **starting position**, and if the sniper moves, say why and what the intention is `S19` ✅. **"If you give up something, take something else"** — e.g. if you left mid for A long, do something at A long; the reason to move should be one the other three can understand. **Call your util** each mode (what you carry for the retake); the setup direction decided: flak jacket, stun, one trophy `S16` 🔶.

✖ **Don't:** Move without a call because "the team will understand."

## 6. The one thing to fix (SnD) `S16` ✅

The team agreed to fix **one mistake per mode.** For SnD those were: **(1)** calling your intention (end point) and **(2)** clearing tires first, then planting. The coach admitted: *"I've been neglecting this game mode for so long because Hardpoint was so much"*, and promised to remind the team before the next scrim ("let's try to make our Coastal a little better").
