## Esports Map Pool

All matches after Stage 2 through the World Championship Finals take place on a map from the Esports Map Pool, split into 3 pools by game mode. The pool is updated yearly by direct vote of World Championship Finals participants (tiebreakers/special circumstances decided by Tournament Administration).

Each year follows a **"Keep 7, Change 6"** rule: 7 maps stay the same, 3 maps rotate to a new mode, and 3 new maps are added. Tournament Administration may adjust the pool mid-year only in exceptional circumstances.

### 1. Hardpoint Map Pool
- Summit
- Hacienda
- Combine
- Takeoff
- Arsenal

### 2. Search & Destroy Map Pool
- Tunisia
- Firing Range
- Coastal

### 3. Control Map Pool

## Mode and Map Order

**Summer Split, Fall Split, and World Championship Finals:** All matches are best-of-5. The higher seed hosts the lobby and ensures the correct map/mode is selected each map.

Order of game modes:
1. Hardpoint
2. Search and Destroy
3. Control
4. Hardpoint
5. Search and Destroy

Map and side selection are determined via a picks-and-bans veto process (below).

## World Championship Veto Process

For Summer Split, Fall Split, and World Championship Finals, map order, side selection, and each player's unique Operator Skill are determined per match via a veto process on a Discord channel provided by Administration.

- All matches except Grand Finals: **Best of 5**
- Grand Finals: **Best of 7**, with a 1-map advantage for the team from Winner's Finals
- The higher seed decides whether to be Team 1 or Team 2

Once a map is banned or picked, it cannot be selected again.

### Veto Process — Best of Five

**Hardpoint Phase**
1. Team 1 bans a Hardpoint map
2. Team 2 bans a Hardpoint map
3. Team 1 picks Map 1; Team 2 picks starting side
4. Team 2 picks Map 4; Team 1 picks starting side

**Search and Destroy Phase**
1. Team 2 bans a S&D map
2. Team 1 bans a S&D map
3. Team 2 picks Map 2; Team 1 picks starting side
4. Team 1 picks Map 5; Team 2 picks starting side

**Control Phase**
1. Team 1 bans a Control map
2. Team 2 bans a Control map
3. Remaining map is Map 3; Team 1 picks starting side

### Veto Process — Grand Finals Best of Seven (with Map Advantage for Winner's Finals team)

**Hardpoint Phase**
1. Team 1 bans a Hardpoint map
2. Team 2 bans a Hardpoint map
3. Team 1 picks Map 1; Team 2 picks starting side
4. Team 2 picks Map 4; Team 1 picks starting side

**Search and Destroy Phase**
1. Team 2 bans a S&D map
2. Team 1 bans a S&D map
3. Team 2 picks Map 2; Team 1 picks starting side
4. Team 1 picks Map 6; Team 2 picks starting side

**Control Phase**
1. Team 1 picks Map 3; Team 2 picks starting side

## Unique Operator Skills

- No two players on the same team may use or have equipped the same Operator Skill at the same time.
- Spawning in with the same Operator Skill as an alive teammate counts as a violation, even without activating it.
- Exception: spawning with the same skill as a *dead* teammate who is mid-switch does **not** count as a violation.
- Players may switch Operator Skills at any time during a map, provided they coordinate to stay compliant.
- **Penalty:** forfeiture of the map on which the violation occurred.

## Loadout Restrictions

Restricted items may not be used at any point during a match beyond the in-game Team Qualifier. Players may only use the allowed Scorestreaks.

> **New content rule:** any newly added functional content (weapons, operator skills, perks, utility, scorestreaks, attachments) is restricted for **3 weeks (21 days)** from its international release, unless Tournament Administration adjusts this window at their discretion. Players should confirm unclear items with Administration before use.

Non-compliance may result in forfeiture of the game/match and/or disqualification. Players are encouraged to record matches as evidence for disputes.

## Weapon Class Roles

For Summer Split, Fall Split, and World Championship Finals, each team assigns **2 weapon class roles per player** from a shared pool of roles (not shared between teams). Roles are declared to opponents and Administration at the start of a series — the team that declared Operator Skills second declares weapon class roles first.

Rules:
- Players may not equip primary weapons outside their assigned roles for the entire series.
- No player may hold two of the same role.
- Roles do **not** apply to secondary weapons or weapons picked up from dead players.
- If a team fails to declare roles promptly, the previous series' roles carry over.

### The 6 Weapon Class Roles
| Role | Grants Access To |
|---|---|
| Assault Rifles | AR weapon class |
| Sub-Machine Guns | SMG weapon class |
| Light-Machine Guns | LMG weapon class |
| Shotgun | Shotgun weapon class |
| Marksman | Marksman Rifle weapon class |
| Sniper | Sniper weapon class |

### Role Pool (per team)
3× AR, 3× SMG, 1× LMG, 1× Shotgun, 1× Marksman, 1× Sniper

**Example declaration:**

|---|---|

| Player 2 | SMG / Shotgun |
| Player 3 | AR / LMG |

## Custom Lobby Creation

### Step 1: Adding Teammates
1. On the home page, click the friends button (top right).
2. Click "Add Friends."
3. Search for a player by their exact Call of Duty: Mobile username (**usernames are case-sensitive**).
   - If the username uses characters you can't type, copy it from an external site like PlayMobile.GG and paste it into the search bar.

### Step 2: Creating Private Matches
1. Click "Multiplayer" on the home page to enter the multiplayer lobby.
2. Click the three-line options icon (top right) → select "Private."
3. You become the host of the private match lobby.
   - Note: you cannot enter a private lobby as a group — only the host creates it and invites others.

### Steps 3 & 4: Adding Teammates & Opponents, Adjusting Settings
1. Use "Invite Friends" (top right) to open the invite panel.
2. Expand the panel and click "+" next to each friend/opponent's name to invite them.
3. Invited players choose their team by clicking an empty box (teammates in your row, opponents in the opposite row), then "unready" themselves as needed.
   - The "Ready" button appears for all non-host players in place of "Start."
4. The host adjusts match settings via the "Match Settings" button (left side) according to the tournament stage's rules.

### Step 5: Set Game Mode and Map
1. Click the game mode button (bottom right) to open map/mode selection.
2. Choose Hardpoint, Search & Destroy, or Control (found under core modes).
3. All 7 maps in the stage's map pool are available for each mode.
4. Select mode and map in the order specified under "Mode and Map Order" (also available on FaceIt for Stage 3).
5. Once mode, map, and settings are set, the host clicks "Start" to begin the match.
