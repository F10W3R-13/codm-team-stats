## Defense

### A) It's a retake map: don't die `S18 · Oct 23` ✅

**Situation:** You defend. The coach sees Firing Range as a **retake map** (A especially; "B site is also retakable").

▶ **Do:** **Keep your numbers before the plant** so a retake is possible — B defenders too. On A site, treat the defense as a retake as well (easy to retake if you use util well). `S16` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Die early: *"if we start to die, it'll make them so easy to specify where the other players are at."*

### B) Mid gamble only with a purpose `S16 · Oct 16` ✅

**Situation:** Someone wants to go mid to look for kills. One round: two players went to mid with no purpose, and both were lost before the retake.

▶ **Do:** Send an SMG to wood **only with a concrete goal** (e.g. a sniper lineup covers mid). **Two ways to retake:** from main, put **5 in front** and delete the enemy by numbers right away (they're spread across the back / wood / up to 3 in front), or **pinch both sides** (through wood, if you have smoke).

✖ **Don't:** Gamble mid with no goal. *(A player pointed out retakes take too long — waiting until 10 s are left — using a Tunisia round as the example; the speaker and the context are unclear.)*

### C) B defense: escape first, kills second `S18` ✅

**Situation:** You defend B (the coach told the defenders mid-scrim to "play safer").

▶ **Do:** **If you're not three in B, be ready to leave B.** Retreat route: the mid ramp is OK, but once the enemy has a power position you get caught on the ramp → **tire side is best** (head glitch). Getting many kills there was good, but **first escape, then kills.** If you see **two or more** enemies: *"wash your hands … just dip it."* `3:26–3:28`

✖ **Don't:** Stay in B to chase a kill when the room is lost.

### D) Same logic: take the ramp first `S19 · Oct 30` ✅

**Situation:** B defense in general (the coach explained a principle, not a specific clip).

▶ **Do:** A is open and **safe to flee** (read info from barrel and drop out of A lane). B has many corners and rooms, so if you're inside and the enemy jumps into B, you can't survive without luck — especially vs strong teams. Of the options, crossfire-killing all five is unrealistic; so: **(1)** get info, then flee to keep numbers and retake; or **(2)** if there's no other way, **proactively take the ramp side before the enemy**, so you can respond from above whatever happens.

✖ **Don't:** Wait inside B for a fight you can't survive.

## Positions and roles

- **Holders must not get trapped in a corner `S16` ✅:** from the ramp, watch prone; if you see a cross, leave and retake *(a player's suggestion — the target player's name is unclear in the captions ❓).* The coach understood that player to be peeking angles to hunt kills.
- **Sniper `S16` ✅:** expect early mid kills, but a sniper can be placed elsewhere — they must **say** "this round I'm going B" so the team adjusts (another player: "if you don't want to go somewhere, tell me and I'll take it").
- **Mid cross hold `S16 · Oct 16` 🔶:** when heading to the trailer, the enemy *Legacy* went through the trailer center almost every time → you need one player watching the center cross from spawn / P1 side. *(That was UD's habit at the time; who holds it was left open.)*
