## P1

**Situation:** You are on P1. Combine P1 is **not** like Summit P1: few hiding / power spots, many entry points (top P3 window side, rock, catwalk). If the enemy holds all the entry points, you can get wiped at once, and firepower can't focus on one spot. `S9` ✅

▶ **Do:** Take the points **outside** that guarantee map control first (green side, catwalk control, head glitch), then come back inside from there. After a 4:1 trade, don't let everyone just help the hill — someone wraps outside to keep numbers and answer spawns (someone must be at catwalk). Actually *take* P1: that is the key of the rotation. `S9 / S5` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Jump inside hunting kills like on Summit P1. Don't give in to the "adrenaline push" — pause. After a trade, hold a corner and let the enemy make the mistake. `S9 / S1 / S5` ✅

**Tricks you can use:**
- **Cinema entry:** start from cinema, throw the smoke far (past the fountain), slip into bottom side, then pinch top + bottom. If you actively control the P1 sides, the open enemies must look down → free kills for the top AR. `S5` ✅
- **E-box:** smoke the enemy's E-box head glitch (a close throw is fine) so they must come out, then kill them from your better position (e.g. top barrel). `S6` ✅
- **Catwalk is not a comfortable spot and is not mandatory** — cycling there is enough. `S6` ✅

### AR on green side `S9` ✅

**Situation:** Green side is the team's agreed priority, and the enemy also prioritizes green (they put blue 3 deep and don't hit the spawn) to pressure it.

▶ **Do:** Take the **angle first.** Green sees a lot of the inside from outside (right of the desk, prone enemies). If a teammate is already in, peek — don't throw your body. If green is the only spawn you have, the enemy comes from both main and deep → prefire. With a War Machine you can hold green and suppress enemies rotating through deep. Killing mid enemies is unnecessary → reposition to green.

✖ **Don't:** Throw your body inside from green.

## P2

**Situation:** First P2 (you take the enemy spawn). `S6` ✅

▶ **Do:** Take the spawn, go deep, then **retake P3 with operators.** Save the War Machine on the first P2 (the coach's opinion). When you use a War Machine to clear corners, push **with** a teammate and tell them where to look (small thing, big difference); clear the corner on the **same side** the enemy comes from first (from the right → right corner first). `S6 / S5` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Run to the hill before getting kills outside. Before entering P2 / P4, get 1–2 kills (usually 1 is enough) from the catwalk side so the enemy spawns outside — then the team can break the hill (river 2 / main 3 pinch). Giving up points for that is OK. `S9` ✅

## P3 ("money hill")

**Situation:** You are on P3 — many entrances, so momentum is hard to use. `S1 / S3` ✅

▶ **Do:** **Top red first** — more so when you have time and space. Four players going deep at the same time is one answer. **Lounge control is key:** one lost gunfight there can mean three enemies spawning at ATM. `S5 / S1 / S3` ✅

✖ **Don't:** Park at lounge worrying about the flank — after a kill, move toward the hill (stay close to your team's spawn). Don't obsess over the deep spawn: it's the money hill, but don't force it. `S5 / S1` ✅

### First P3 vs second P3 `S6` ✅

**Situation:** You are at P2 → P3 and you have operators.

▶ **Do:** **First P3:** take the spawn + go deep + retake with operators. **Second P3:** the enemy is afraid of the spawn take and puts people on the spawn → slam from the front and push with operators. If the first P3 worked, the default for the second P3 is the opposite (front) — unless you have no operators.

✖ **Don't:** Play scared on the second P3 — even at 2v5, pull the operator and go in (sacrifice / promise).

### First P3: take the spawn side first `S16 · Oct 16` ✅

**Situation:** First P3 with operators. *(The transcript had no map name; the coach confirmed on Oct 17 that it was Combine P3.)*

▶ **Do:**
- **Take the spawn side first.** If you slam first P3 from main, the enemy turns on an operator *inside* and farms kills; from main you have no distance and the view into the hill isn't open. From the spawn side you have **long sight lines** and can pour Sparrow / War Machine / Claw into the hill with little risk. The aim is to project firepower efficiently — not "to have the spawn".
- Can't get deep? Putting 5 deep first is OK. If it fails, take the spaces **one at a time.**
- Entering: take **window side first, but don't advance more than you need** (the enemy in the window room hopes you peek). **Window side + staircase side together** are the base; secret side is a bonus (you can be hit from the hill and from the enemy barrel). The coach agreed to *"try top and window first — an experiment is not bad"*.
- Entering from main: there are many entrances (secret / top / window). Alone? Don't peek — take the entrances **one at a time**; the easiest from main is window side.
- Goal: make the enemy's operator **useless** and kill first.

✖ **Don't:** Slam main just because "going deep is risky" — the coach said the worry is valid, but it doesn't mean slamming main. If you take deep and still can't take P4, that's a **separate problem**: you are too passive when you push. 

### After a 3-kill: don't expand, solidify `S11 · Sep 17` 🔶 *(map not confirmed)*

▶ **Do:** Re-measure the value of killing players who fight the hill **from outside**: killing someone who just arrived after a long run sends them back to spawn and gives teammates time to think (double benefit). Expect the enemy's first-wave trick: showing their body before the setup is perfect so you think you can enter — then hiding.

✖ **Don't:** Count on killing a just-spawned player — little value (same logic as 16 s inside vs 4 s outside). Don't take a "50/50 gamble" forward after a 3-kill: *"we need to solidify, not to expand."*

## P4

**Situation:** You are on P4. Spawn does no magic here. `S3` ✅

▶ **Do:** **Control the staircase** — it limits the enemy's only option to main.

✖ **Don't:** Trust the spawn to do the work for you.

## Any hill

- **Habit check `S11` 🔶:** after killing one enemy, running out because "there's one more" is a *finger habit*, not a brain decision — the coach framed it as a habit to fix, not a personal blame.
- **Window side vs head glitch (AR) `S9` 🔶 *(hill not specified; captions may be wrong)*:** when leaving spawn, use **window side more often.** Against enemies already inside, that head glitch is weak and ~75 % of your body is exposed to inner / river; at window side, the amount of body exposed is the same at any peek angle, so it's predictable.
