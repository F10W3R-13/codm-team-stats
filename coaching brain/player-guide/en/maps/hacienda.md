## P1

**Situation:** P1 on Hacienda — you are choosing between **ping** (삥) and **smoke**. `S7` ✅

▶ **Do:** Throw a **smoke and dive** — that's the standard. Watch mostly the **2nd-floor routes.** If you have no smoke and want to clear a narrow area with the ping, go up to the 2nd floor and use it there.

✖ **Don't:** Rely on the ping to survive here: it is mainly a personal-gunfight helper, **not a tool for joining the team**. And its value comes from *dying a lot* (it refills on death) — if you get many kills, it's worth less. On the 1st floor the ping doesn't fit (the fountain makes the angles wide).

## P2

### A) Two spawns: rock side vs statue side `S17` ✅

**Situation:** Both spawns (rock and statue) are possible — you must decide which one to take.

▶ **Do:** Decide by **information.** If you spot the rock side, **call which side you're looking at** so teammates can decide. If you're isolated between the two spawns, don't hesitate: call your status. If you get a good advantage, **take all you can, then step back and use rock as your second chance.**

✖ **Don't:** Expect to hold **rock and statue for a full 60 s** at the same time — it's impossible; it will break eventually.

### B) You have rock side but the main slam comes `S17` ✅

**Situation:** You hold rock side and keep trading, but the enemy smokes main and stacks numbers to slam.

▶ **Do:** Sometimes push **white side to flank** — you don't need to worry about enemies coming from the water side. Go **one step ahead**, not late. If you're winning on kills, you can push white side (and a little sideways) as long as you earn points. The flank's goal is **to return to the hill**, not to take the driveway.

✖ **Don't:** Burn **4 operators** and then just hold a corner — that's a waste: *"we have to get full 60."* Push when the situation is clear (you know where they are) and you can **call it**. A half-hearted push in one direction gets you caught before the driveway.

### C) What to prioritize at this stage `S12 · Sep 23` ✅

**Situation:** You hold the advantage (3 operators, lots of kills). The coach's priority is **driveway side + statue control.**

▶ **Do:** Keep the plan simple. White side isn't a big threat right now.

✖ **Don't:** Farm kills at white side — killing someone there has little value and moves the spawn toward statue side, which complicates things. *(This is "for now": the coach said high-level teams' ways aren't wrong, but at this stage we cut the complicated farming. A player disagreed — see the last section.)*

## P3

### A) Util and entry `S7` ✅

**Situation:** P3 is a hill with narrow paths.

▶ **Do:** Put your grenades **inside the hill.** If you set a ping up top, go that way; if a grenade is behind the fence, prefire as it pops and walk in — **design the engagement.** Ping direction and body direction must match ("different dreams" = they didn't).

✖ **Don't:** Throw grenades outside the hill (balcony / water) — that's fear of the enemy's slay. Don't burn all your util and then enter on pure aim / luck: you die.

### B) Operators, equalizer, window side `S17 · Oct 21` ✅ *(coach confirmed the map: Hacienda)*

**Situation:** You're defending or retaking P3 with operators.

▶ **Do:** Use operators **inside.** The enemy **equalizer user** is the one to bombard with the War Machine — *"I don't want this guy to be alive inside of the hill at the corner."* The enemy tries to clear window side, so **peek from outside the hill** to avoid the explosives (this time Sparrow; next time maybe a War Machine). Go for **one clean push** with the operators. **Even with only 20 s left, invest in P3 to the end** — it pushes them down to the deep spawn and makes the P4 rotation easier.

✖ **Don't:** Stack the operators with no clear purpose (the coach understood the idea, but wants it clearer next time). Don't sit in a spot you will die in anyway.

### C) AR firepower is being wasted `S17` ✅

**Situation:** Both ARs are on top plat and the enemy fills the hill with smoke.

▶ **Do:** Move **one** AR to another route (e.g. **office**): the SMGs shift toward office too, so they can drop to top plat, shoot down, or flank con. Spread the firepower over *different routes / angles*.

✖ **Don't:** Keep two ARs on the same top plat blocked by smoke — in practice that's 3–4 players of firepower in a 5-person hill. *(The coach asked one player to try this role first; if it doesn't work, use office more instead of E-box.)*

## P4

### A) Which corner to clear first `S5` ✅

**Situation:** You know where you're coming from (the spawn is confirmed).

▶ **Do:** Clear the corner on the **same side first** — from the left, the left corner (that angle is naturally visible; the opposite corner stays uncleared). An angle "gamble" (left or right?) is OK **to clear P4**.

✖ **Don't:** Stretch out to spawn-kill (slay out) without hill control: little value, and the enemy inside the hill (head glitch + trophy) pushes you out. A gamble *for a slay out* has low value.

### B) Clear the corners even if you hold the hill; say what you are doing `S18 · Oct 23` ✅ *(coach confirmed Hacienda P4 on Oct 24)*

**Situation:** You have the hill, the cut-off and the spawn, so it feels safe.

▶ **Do:** **Still clear corners** (kitchen, water…) — an enemy rat may be there. Be aware of the **route** you take to the hill. If you're doing a rat play, **say it:** *"I'm not talking about teammates helping you. I'm talking about you being here so that our teammates can focus on their own thing."* Your job is to say what you're doing; what teammates do next is on them.

✖ **Don't:** Run to the same corner because you didn't know a teammate was already there (it wastes the team). Don't call the same spot by two names — **top balcony = top plat**. And don't let one 3-kill rat game make "ignore the hill" your style: the coach says those plays "are not supposed to be working".

### C) Spawn pushed back, and after two kills `S17` ✅

**Situation:** You start P4 with the spawn pushed back; or you just got two kills.

▶ **Do:** Starting with the spawn behind you is not a disaster — the coach said you did well, because the enemy was indecisive: they should have pushed to deep side while they could, and got pinched. Don't hesitate when the chance comes (a hesitation would have cost two kills). **After two kills, assume you're about to be hunted** and move at once (push on or fall back).

✖ **Don't:** Stay at the position where you got the kills.

### D) 2nd P4: numbers + operators but pinned `S16 · Oct 16` ✅ *(coach confirmed Hacienda 2nd P4 on Oct 17)*

**Situation:** You have operators (Claw / Tempest / War Machine) and the numbers, yet you can't take the corner and the enemy contests the hill.

▶ **Do:** Make a **"string them out" call** — the enemy should be the one pinned. UD swings straight away when they have numbers inside, otherwise throws smoke. We look for outside kills **on purpose**.

✖ **Don't:** Swing alone (it ended in a death and the hill broke too easily), and then everybody slams main in tunnel vision.

## Any hill

- **Don't solve what you read by killing `S10` ✅:** you read the spawn correctly — the problem is not being able to *ignore* it and pushing into a zone you're confident in to find a kill. Example: we spawn at water side and have no rock side → **no rock flank**; **slam the hill first**, and if you have driveway side, clean white side from inside. If you spawn at the driveway → lobby push → white side, you leave driveway empty: return to driveway at once or join the hill. A single kill must not become the goal. Throw smoke on that table and **run, so the enemy chases you and your team catches them.**
- **Inside the hill first `S10` ✅:** if the hill is blocked and the enemy keeps contesting, drop what you were thinking and look at the hill (not water side). An enemy left inside won't die from wrapping alone — **one player goes in, with numbers, prefires and clears** before you look for other value. Beware of "contesting just to contest".
- **Smoke mindset `S17` ✅:** "throw smoke → win the gunfight with a clear view." If you only try to *occupy an area* with smoke, you die to corner enemies. Keep throwing smoke toward the enemy's deep side. Worried about a prefire from E-box? Prefire too: *"if they can see you, then you can see him."*
- **Operators (meta as of Session 17, Oct 21) `S17` ✅:** **Claw** works well in **P2 and P4** defense (not much value in P3). **Tempest** works better used **inside** the hill than cut from outside, especially against enemies coming from white side (shock) — it ruins UD's "stack and hit the hill with numbers".
- **Don't reveal you rotated first `S17` ✅:** sitting in hill corners makes you predictable; moving like a rat avoids that.
- **Defend vs push out `S11 · Sep 17` 🔶 *(map not confirmed)*:** both are right, as long as **you don't die fast.** For now: stay in the hill (unless the enemy throws crazy smoke), throw the trophy in front to block their smoke, and peek with a positional advantage while it's open, so they can't enter even without a driveway push. You'll sometimes get punished — that's accepted. Goal by stage 5: have **both**. *"Don't look for the ultimate answer; take one step at a time."*
