# Hardpoint (HP) — General Principles

> 📌 This guide is **extracted from Coach Flower's session transcripts** — a summary, not new rules. The coach's advice depends on **map, hill, situation, opponent and timing**: read the *Situation* line first.

**Confidence:** ✅ coach said it · 🔶 inferred / not confirmed by the coach yet — treat as "maybe" · ❓ unconfirmed — **not an instruction** (see the last section).
**Tags:** `S19` = Session 19 (review of a past scrim). Dates are shown where the knowledge base has them.

> First to **250**. Hold a fixed hill (P1 → P4) and stack time. These are the principles that hold across maps; **map-specific conditions are in each map page** (Takeoff, Combine, Hacienda, Summit, Arsenal, Standoff). Player-by-player notes are in the team notes.

---

## 1. Rotation and spawn

**Situation:** You are deciding when and how to rotate, or whether to chase the spawn.

▶ **Do:** *Spawn is a tendency, not the answer* — **actually taking P1 is the key of the rotation** `S5`. Rotate early to the next **money hill** (hills differ in importance). **Rotation is a promised priority — the more you're behind, the earlier you go:** if you hunt kills while behind, the team splits; if you go first, the team stays together and gets a setup to turn it around. If you're ahead on points and the next hill is hard to break, giving up the whole scrap is fine `S18`. **First P3** → take the spawn; **second P3** → the enemy prepares for the spawn, so force the front with operators `S6`. Spawn rules differ per hill (Summit P3: many inside → spawn first; Summit P1: keep flooding; Arsenal P1: 5 inside is over-investment; Combine P1: outside map control; Combine P3: spawn side first with operators) `S15 · S16`.

✖ **Don't:** Trust the deep spawn blindly and neglect the hill `S1`. The first player to rotate shouldn't go to the next hill alone — the enemy reads it `S5`.

## 2. Inside the hill

**Situation:** You hold or are contesting a hill.

▶ **Do:** **Create value inside the hill.** Slay out **only after** you have hill control `S5`. **Recycle = always go in, even if teammates are already inside** — by the time you hear a call or see the OBJ die, it's late `S16`. **Shoulder to shoulder** alignment. Keep your first intention. **Before breaking an enemy hill**, one kill outside can be leverage even if you give up some points `S9` (whether to take outside map control first depends on the map: Summit P1 inside / Combine P1 outside). **First wave on P4:** the player who rotated first defends **inside**, not outside `S6`.

✖ **Don't:** Cycle out (leave the hill for outside kills). Watch every angle at once — pick one `S6 · S9`. Slay out without hill control `S5`. Don't push at once after a kill (momentum can reverse). Don't stay at the spot where you got a first kill: your position is revealed — play safe first, leave when the team is close, **hunt a called enemy at once** `S16`.

---

## 3. Operators

**Situation:** You hold or can get an operator skill (War Machine, Claw, Tempest, Equalizer…). *Meta changes with time — see the weapons notes when available.*

▶ **Do:** **Call the operators you hold** — especially at P2 → P3 `S15`. Use an operator with a **sacrifice / promise** — someone enters as a trade, agreed beforehand, so you don't need a call each time `S6`. **See, then pop** (confirm before using). While defending, invest operators **inside** first so the enemy can't earn time `S10`. **Claw** works in P2 / P4 defense; **Tempest / Equalizer** inside `S17`.

✖ **Don't:** Call "I'm using it" and not use it (the team adjusts and loses value; trust drops) `S9`. Hold an operator and not use or call it `S15`. Pour in four operators and then just hold a corner: *"we have to get full 60"* — if the situation is clear, push the enemy out, but only with a call and a plan to come back `S17`.

## 4. Team coordination

**Situation:** The team has to act as one.

▶ **Do:** **Push only on a signal** from a chosen player (e.g. an SKS one-tap); once the signal comes, go **immediately** `S15 · S16`. **Swing together, on time:** late one-by-one swings lose to flooding `S16`. **Say what you're about to do** (contest, flank, rotation, break) — it's a statement, not an order; it lets teammates put firepower in the same place and not run over for nothing `S18`. If you're isolated between spawns, **call what you see** `S17`. Read your teammate's **stamina:** a player who did a lot alone is about to fall — fill in at once `S9`. After a teammate's 3-kill, help them `S13`. **One mistake per mode:** for HP it's **rotation** — just one thing, done consistently `S16`.

✖ **Don't:** Swing alone and die with no signal `S15`. Split intentions when the team has agreed (e.g. the "3-kill → enter" moment) `S9`. Do the rotation/break/contest without saying it, then blame it on "common sense" afterwards `S18`. Use smoke only where it's comfortable for you — there is **no team agreement** on smoke yet `S17`. Don't make the same-spot, every-angle prefire: **pick one** `S9`.

---

## 5. Common mistakes (checklist)

- Cycling out; single entry ("too confident with gunfight"); slay out with no hill control.
- Not doing the agreed rotation (tunnel vision, "we already talked about it").
- Running off after a kill with no plan; leaving a team that is playing well.
- Fighting for CDOM / snow / top P2 as a destination instead of supporting the hill `S16`.
- Not clearing corners because you "have the hill" `S18`.
- Isolated players not calling what they see `S17`.
