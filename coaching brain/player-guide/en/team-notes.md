## Maozyn — AR / sniper (Spanish speaker)

- **Style:** The coach's point is the **habit of moving like an SMG** (wall slide, ADS advance) — not aim. "An AR shouldn't die easily." Good baseline for helping the team up close; makes decisions fast. Tool: record your POV and self-review — *"Habit will be fixed if you're being conscious about it."* `S9 · S10`
- **Positions:** Catwalk isn't mandatory; Maozyn is good at close-range gunnies, so can create value at inner / river instead (Combine). `S6`
- **Before entering P2 / P4 (Combine):** getting killed by outside enemies while rushing to the hill; the coach wants one outside kill first. Also "too support-minded — AR firepower is wasted." `S9`
- **Summit:** keep holding computer side as an AR support line `S15`. Praised: 2-piece kills and the Equalizer getting better `S15`; cleaning green side, playing bottom connector well, helping the hill while doing the snow job `S17`.
- **Hacienda P3:** first assigned the **office-route flank** (two ARs on top plat waste firepower) `S17`.
- **SnD:** the sniper's essence is suppression (don't push kitchen alone, Raid) `S8`. At Coastal the mid → A long → mid indecision was pointed out `S19`. The coach's read of the passive play: **English calls aren't always possible (~40 %) and playstyle (~60 %)** — a diagnosis of structure, not ability. A sniper is used **when requested**, with the starting position shared `S19`. From the Oct 22 scrim review: on **4–5 player pushes, Maozyn watches behind** `S18`.
- **Habits as structure:** pre-using a War Machine on a guess was a habit → "see, then pop" `S6`.
- **Language:** talks with Polar in Spanish `S18`.
