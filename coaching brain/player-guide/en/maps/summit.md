# Summit · Hardpoint

> 📌 This guide is **extracted from Coach Flower's session transcripts** — a summary, not new rules. The coach's advice depends on **map, hill, situation, opponent and timing**: read the *Situation* line first.

**Confidence:** ✅ coach said it · 🔶 inferred / not confirmed by the coach yet — treat as "maybe" · ❓ unconfirmed — **not an instruction** (see the last section).
**Tags:** `S19` = Session 19 (review of a past scrim). Dates are shown where the knowledge base has them.

> ⚠️ **Callouts: only CDOM is defined.** **CDOM** = the zone that matches the "C" point of Domination mode (coach confirmed Oct 17); captions often write it "seed / seat / sedum / C-dom". Not defined: computer side (captions: "controller / server side"), snow side, green side, top P2, bottom P2, main, forklift (fork), connector (con), staircase, window, cage, rock, deep secret grid… Use the names the team uses; ask Flower if unsure.

## P1

### A) Inside the hill `S9` ✅

**Situation:** You are on P1. Unlike Combine P1, the hill has many power positions inside (corner, under the cage, staircase head glitch), so you **can find kills inside.**

▶ **Do:** Prefire everything, even if the AR has little to do at first. **Both ARs go for computer side at the same time** — one may die, the other survives, so one is always there. Even if you think an AR is already there, **call "I take computer"** and don't wander off. Keep the War Machine for P2 / P3 (or a desperate moment).

✖ **Don't:** Spend the War Machine on P1: it's an over-investment (trophies are on top main / connector, almost none inside, and SMG firepower inside is already enough).

### B) Computer side support line `S15 · Oct 13` ✅

**Situation:** An AR is already visible at computer side.

▶ **Do:** **Keep holding computer side anyway** so the AR line never breaks (support line). If nobody is there, someone must do it.

### C) Keep flooding the hill `S16` ✅

**Situation:** P1 is tight and the hill keeps breaking even with many players inside.

▶ **Do:** Summit P1 is **not a hill where you can hold the spawn**, so even with everybody inside, **keep flowing into the hill and defend inside** (staying together lets you help at once without relying on the minimap). Give the **final blow** to the last enemy.

✖ **Don't:** Go outside to cut someone when the hill is already full. The enemy's pattern: one player prone in smoke buys time while another keeps throwing their body in. Our disease: *"we're already ahead on kills, a teammate will do it"* → leave for outside → final blow never comes.

### D) After the first kill from top P2 `S16` ✅

**Situation:** You got a first kill from top P2 and held the position.

▶ **Do:** Play safe first. Your position is revealed — **when the team gets close, abandon it and jump down** (enemies come to hunt you). Check hop-up, P2 main, below the hill and the staircase side. When a hunt call comes out, **hunt at once.**

### E) Top P2 is a route, not a goal `S16 · S17` ✅

**Situation:** You're deciding how much to invest in top P2, CDOM or snow during P1.

▶ **Do:** Use top P2 as a **checkpoint** you pass through. Final blow comes **before** map control. Top P2 gives you information, not a fortress. If you're at top and torn between peeking staircase and shooting down the hill from window, **pick one — if split, choose window toward the hill:** kill one, then jump down away from the top enemy. On P1 prefer **bottom side** more often than top P2.

✖ **Don't:** Overvalue top P2: part of its value is an **illusion** (30–40 % of it), and that alone is enough for the enemy to break P1. Don't sit alone at top watching other lanes instead of helping the hill. Don't clean CDOM / snow as if it were a destination.

## P2

### A) Reading the spawn `S9 · Oct 8` ✅

**Situation:** Possible spawns: CDOM (top) / green / snow. If everyone starts at CDOM spawn, you can only hope to win the gunfight by numbers.

▶ **Do:** Coming from snow spawn **and** top at the same time makes a pinch on main + dom. When heading to main side, **check what the team is doing in P2 and stop** — help if you can; if not, wrap to snow and come back. Stopping to prepare the gunfight is fine. Use **jiggle peek** with an AR.

✖ **Don't:** Slide peek with nobody behind you (free kill for them). Don't chase a trade kill when the team's intention is to enter — **after your teammate's 3 kills, enter the hill at once** (hip fire push): split intentions split the team's thinking. Main side has no power positions or head glitches, and the enemy has them all: don't blindly commit there.

*Weapons note (meta as of S9, Oct 8): an AR can beat an SMG on aim when the SMG is the Switchblade type (not USS 9 / Hurricane).*

### B) Valuing yourself too much `S16` ✅

**Situation:** You're tempted to go outside (snow push, CDOM slay out).

▶ **Do:** **AR: take top P2 and pull the enemy forward** while SMGs spot from green side — don't stand behind aiming at the War Machine. P2 needs points *now*, not P3 rotation prep. **Recycle = go in even if someone is already inside** — by the time you hear the call or see the OBJ die, it's too late. Ahead with 10 s to win and the enemy panic-pushes? **Don't hold power positions — slam from the front and enter corners properly.**

✖ **Don't:** *"You are valuing yourself too much."* Don't try to do everything (watch CDOM + counter flank + support the hill) — *"trying to do everything, end up doing nothing."* Don't die throwing a main smoke for an enemy who has nowhere to hide. If the enemy retakes and slams inside (copying Elevate), play from outside; don't just throw your body.

### C) You lose top P2 `S17` ✅

**Situation:** The enemy has top P2 and the hill changed to P2.

▶ **Do:** You can take it back: the enemy only reaches top P2 through the staircase; **you** have the hop up, going down to window side, etc. If you spawn at CDOM side, your first job is **a kill** — slow, with a peek, no "I'll take that space" — then retake from top and bottom. Holding the P2-side hill also makes green pressure easy.

✖ **Don't:** Be anxious about losing top P2 on P1.

## P3

### A) Hill recycle `S12 · Sep 23` ✅

**Situation:** The enemy breaks in from main side every time, and we allow it too easily (two players idle at green side holding spawn, four stacked from main).

▶ **Do:** **Spawn holders also get ready to recycle the hill** — don't stay still because you have a power position. Summit P3 is so obvious that you must **move before the call.** Keep players flowing into the hill. Watch the **midline and the minimap** equally. If the enemy flanks, kill them inside and retake the spawn. 2nd P3: when the hill is empty, enter with stopping power and keep profiting with prefire / nade / smoke inside.

✖ **Don't:** Try to contest this hill from outside (unlike P1): whoever is inside has the advantage, whichever spawn. The enemy burns 20–30 points contesting but gains time to rotate to P4 first and P2 points — **we must hold this hill no matter what.** *(Deep spawn is a big long-term advantage, but its value shows against teams stronger than UD.)*

### B) Break P3 vs defend P3 `S15 · S16` ✅

**Situation:** At P2 → P3 with operators.

▶ **Do:** **Call the operators you hold** ("especially the operators"). *Breaking* first P3: operators are likely all in hand → **stack them and slam main like UD**; second P3: operators are random → approach with **map control** (green side, spawn first). *Defending* with many players inside: take the spawn first, then watch the hill (the "obvious power spawn" rule).

✖ **Don't:** Mix up the two: the "stack and slam main" rule is for **attacking**; the "spawn first" rule is for **defending.** `S16` coach-confirmed Oct 17

## P4

### A) Defend the first wave inside `S4 · S6` ✅

**Situation:** First wave on P4 (spawn is important).

▶ **Do:** The player who rotated first defends the first wave **inside the hill.** Put the War Machine on P2.

✖ **Don't:** Defend from outside (mid players are timing-dependent and hard to read).

### B) Bad spawn: main side and the forklift guy `S9` ✅

**Situation:** P4 with a bad spawn. AR has no comfortable spot (main, snow, stairs are all awkward).

▶ **Do:** **1)** get information first (jiggle peek, or a quick rush that doesn't expose you long); **2)** **tie them down** — show your body without dying so the forklift guy keeps looking at you while teammates move on snow side; **3)** with luck, a laser kill. Practice challenging the forklift guy **even at 10 % win chance** — smoke and prayer don't work; only an AR can do it. Snow side: you can hold angles from deep secret grid and rock. As attacker, you choose when to push. With 3 kills and wide spawn space, remember P4 is a spawn-control hill — and check your teammates aren't wasting firepower.

✖ **Don't:** Treat pushing main as an option — fight.

### C) First wave: go deep, one intention `S16 · S17` ✅

**Situation:** First P4 wave; the team agreed **"go deep, clear deep, think deep".**

▶ **Do:** **Pick one intention and commit** (clear the hill *or* wrap to CDOM). Clearing the hill needs a **simultaneous entry.** "Go snow" = a call to avoid the enemy cutting from top — **not everyone has to go.** First wave: **deep first** — give up forklift / snow if you have to; kill one enemy there first so they think their spot is safe and can't use an operator. The fork isn't dangerous on the first wave; it becomes dangerous after the first wave, when we hold the spawn. Place players where you can win the gunfight. **Textbook play:** hold the staircase so the enemy can't use CDOM side, kill one, then go up at once to help another lane.

✖ **Don't:** Swing alone because an enemy is there — swing **because of the agreement.** Don't overvalue snow: it's one option, not their main entrance, and the enemy pays a lot to use it. Last time we took the first spawn but got only 14 points (the coach: "bad") — snow overvalued and both teams invested operators.

### D) Answering UD's slam `S16` ✅

**Situation:** Four enemies rush one position, abandon their spots and slam the hill. Their goal is to kill *that one player* (final blow first).

▶ **Do:** **Don't answer the same way — wrap outside when they slam**, if there is time. P4 is a hill where you can score inside without the spawn (unlike P3 — UD also takes spawn at P3).

✖ **Don't:** Wrap with 15 s left — it's too late (they're already in score-securing mode).

## Any hill

- **Snow obsession `S15` ✅:** once you get a first kill by going deep to snow, **that kill is the last value snow gives you.** Staying there leaves the hill empty, teammates scattered outside, the enemy flooding the hill and prefiring your equalizer position. Go help near the hill / container side.
- **Equalizer inside `S17` ✅:** when a teammate is using / about to use the equalizer inside, the others assume they do it and the SMGs **don't push far outside** — support them inside.
- **Operators `S15` ✅:** "the comp must match the operators you hold." *(In one scrim the team held 5 operators — War Machine, Claw… — and barely used them: "the first wave was ruined".)*
