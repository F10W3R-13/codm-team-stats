## Control

### Stuck at spawn `S5` ✅

**Situation:** Your team struggles to leave the spawn, and 1–2 teammates are roaming in the enemy's territory.

▶ **Do:** **Capture the site that roaming player took.** When you capture it, the enemies attacking your spawn have to look behind them → your team escapes the spawn and **starts from there.** (This ties to the principle "use your potential value for the team.")

✖ **Don't:** Leave the roaming player's progress alone while the team stays pinned at spawn.
